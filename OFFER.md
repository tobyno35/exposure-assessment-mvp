# Vulnerability Exposure and Remediation Assessment

**$150 flat introductory fee**

Get a prioritized list of exposure concerns for the systems you choose, with evidence references and practical steps for you or your IT provider.

## What’s included

- Review of saved evidence you provide for up to five named business systems.
- Three checks per system where evidence is available. The review covers internet-reachable administrative access, disabled MFA for administrative access, and software reported as out of vendor support.
- One Markdown report with a summary, approved asset list, findings by severity, evidence references, recommended actions, and any checks with insufficient evidence.
- One round of email clarification on the delivered report within seven calendar days of delivery.

## What we need from you

Provide a named contact authorized to approve the review, the list of systems to include, and dated, redacted screenshots or saved configuration exports relevant to those systems. We agree on which evidence can be shared and how to transfer it before work starts.

## Scope and timing

Delivery is within **three business days** after written approval of the scope and receipt of agreed evidence. The fixed fee covers one review of the approved systems and the report. Live scanning, penetration testing, independent verification, incident response, compliance certification, remediation work, and retesting are outside the offer.

To get started, reply with the systems you’d like reviewed. We’ll confirm the scope and send the evidence checklist before you share any files.
